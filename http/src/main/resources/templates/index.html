Wallet Backend

Welcome to the A-SIT Plus Wallet System. This is the Backend, which orchestrates issuing of credentials and keeps track of the validity of issued credentials.

The OpenAPI spec for this service is available at /v3/api-docs, and the Swagger UI at /swagger-ui/index.html. Note that access to these resources may be restricted.

We encourage you to take a tour through the Wallet Demo!

  1. Install Apps
    Download and install the Wallet App from above on your smartphone.
    Optionally: Download and install the Verifier App on a second smartphone.
  2. Biometric Authentication
    During the creation of the device binding, you'll need to confirm the biometric authentication on your smartphone.
  3. Load Credentials
    In your Wallet App, click on “Load Credentials” or the plus sign to load a PupilId into the Wallet.
  4. Review Credentials
    The Wallet App shows a preview of the credentials contained in the PupilId. Confirm the credentials to save them onto your smartphone.
  5. Present Credentials
    In your Wallet App, click on your PupilId to present the credentials for verification.
  6. Validate Credentials
    On your second smartphone, open the Verifier App and scan the QR code presented on the Wallet App.
  7. Revoke Credentials
    Go to the Revoke page to revoke your credentials. Afterwards, a verification of the credentials from your Wallet App shall fail.
